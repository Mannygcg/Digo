What Digo does

Six reasons to stop typing

Speak anywhere

One shortcut wakes Digo from the menu bar, in any app — your text editor, your inbox, your terminal.

Cleans up as you go

Filler words and stutters get quietly trimmed. Punctuation and casing happen automatically.

Stays on your Mac

Transcription runs on-device, so nothing you say ever leaves your laptop to get there.

Learns your words

Teach Digo names, jargon, and acronyms once, and it stops guessing every time after.

One tap to start

Control + Space by default, or a click on the menu bar icon. Change the shortcut anytime in Settings.

Built for real talk

Handles pauses, tangents, and long rambles without losing the thread of what you meant.
